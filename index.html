Trade Exit Guide
Can I exit whenever I want?
Yes — two of the three exit routes are your choice. The trailing stop exits automatically. The 5-day rule and profit target exits are manual. Never exit out of fear when the trade is going your way.
My 3 valid exits
Exit 1 — Trailing stop triggered (automatic): Platform closes trade and locks in profit. Best exit — no emotion involved.

Exit 2 — Profit target reached (your choice): Target % hit. Close manually. Take the profit.

Exit 3 — 5-day rule (your choice): 5 trading days elapsed. Close at market price. The edge has expired.
