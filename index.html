❌ SELL SIGNAL
Price touches upper band
RSI above 60 (confirms)
Price closes back inside band
Target: middle band (20 SMA)
💡 How Bollinger Bands work:

The bands expand and contract based on market volatility. Price naturally returns to the middle band (average) after touching the outer bands — this is mean reversion.

Upper band = 20-period average + 2 standard deviations — statistically overbought
Middle band = 20-period average — your profit target
Lower band = 20-period average - 2 standard deviations — statistically oversold

Combined with RSI = much stronger signal. Band touch alone = 55% win rate. Band touch + RSI confirmation = 62-70% win rate.
Key rules: Target = middle band (not a fixed % move) · Stop = ATR-based below/above the band touched · Max hold = 4 hours (1 candle cycle) · Exit immediately if price closes outside the band (signal failed) · Best during London (08:00-16:00 UK) and NY (14:30-21:00 UK) sessions · Avoid 00:00-07:00 UK — low volume, unreliable bands
