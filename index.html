📦 Breakout Retest — Daily bias, 1H box, 15m entry
Markets: you choose below (default EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY). Each market costs 3 credits to load and about 5 credits an hour while this tab is open, so scan only the ones you will actually trade.
1 · Bias (morning, Daily chart): ignore today's unfinished candle. Take the last completed Daily close and compare it with the 50 SMA (above = buy, below = sell), then check the last 3 swing highs and lows (buys: each higher than the one before; sells: each lower). Both must agree — if not, skip the day.
2 · Setup (1H): a tight 12-candle box, then a big candle that closes strongly outside it in the bias direction. The zone must be fresh (not retested several times) and price must pull back to the broken edge — top edge for buys, bottom for sells. Middle of the box = skip.
3 · Entry (15m): a 15m candle rejects the edge and closes in the trade direction. Stop just beyond the far side of the box (+ buffer), target 2x the stop, risk 1% of capital.
Skip: 22:00–22:59 UK (rollover spread), outside 07:00–21:59 UK (this window suits forex; indices have their own hours, so check them too), price closed through the whole zone, stop wider than 3x the 1H ATR.
