⚡ RSI Mean Reversion Signals
A complementary strategy to Larry Williams — fires more frequently, documented 68-75% win rate on major forex pairs over 20+ years.
💡 How This Strategy Works:

RSI below 30 = market oversold = price likely to bounce = BUY signal
RSI above 70 = market overbought = price likely to fall = SELL signal

Trend filter: Only buy when price is above the 50-day SMA. Only sell when price is below. This eliminates low-quality signals.

Entry: Next day open after RSI crosses the threshold at daily close
Stop: Beyond the recent swing high (for sells) or swing low (for buys)
Target: When RSI returns to 50 (neutral zone) — typically 1-3 days
Hold: Maximum 3 trading days
⚠ Important Difference From Larry Williams:
This strategy does NOT use COT data or seasonal patterns. It fires purely on price momentum returning to average. Use it when Larry Williams has no green signals. Never run both strategies on the same market at the same time.

📄 How to verify RSI yourself on TradingView (free):

1. Go to tradingview.com and search your market (e.g. GBPUSD)
2. Select Daily (1D) timeframe
3. Click Indicators and search RSI — add it with period 14
4. Look at the RSI value at the bottom:
   Below 30 = oversold = valid BUY
   Above 70 = overbought = valid SELL
   Between 30-70 = no signal, stand aside

Always check at market close — never mid-session.
