📈 4H SMA Pullback Strategy

Signal: 20 SMA > 50 SMA, both rising, price pulls back to 20 SMA, bullish confirmation candle closes → enter next candle.
Stop: Beyond recent swing high/low, checked against ATR(14).
Target: Minimum 2:1 reward:risk. Trail stop after profit.
Check: 4-hour candle closes at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 UK.

⚡ 5/10 Momentum Trigger — 4H

The 5/10 crossover is not the signal — it is the trigger. The trade decision is based on trend quality first. The cross simply tells you momentum may be returning.

Decision based on: Is the 50 SMA rising? Is the 20 SMA rising? Higher highs and higher lows? Orderly pullback? Convincing candle? No major news?
Trigger: 5 SMA crosses 10 SMA in the direction of the trend = momentum returning = enter next candle.

⚠ Need 4 of 6 checks green before entering. The cross alone is never enough.
