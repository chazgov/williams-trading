📈 4H EMA Pullback Strategy

Signal: 20 EMA > 50 EMA, both rising, price pulls back to 20 EMA, bullish confirmation candle closes → enter next candle.
Stop: Beyond recent swing high/low, checked against ATR(14).
Target: Minimum 2:1 reward:risk. Trail stop after profit.
When to check: 08:05, 12:05, 16:05, 20:05 UK (5 mins after each 4H candle closes). Signal valid until the next 4H candle close. Do not chase — if you miss the entry candle, wait for the next signal.

⚡ 9 EMA Momentum Trigger — 1H

Uses the 1-hour chart for more frequent signals. The 9 EMA cross/hook above the 20 EMA is the trigger. The trade decision is based on trend quality first.

Decision based on: Is the 20 EMA rising? Is the 9 EMA rising? Higher highs and higher lows? Orderly pullback? Convincing candle? No major news?
Trigger: 9 EMA hooks or crosses above 20 EMA (for buys) after pullback = momentum returning = enter next 1H candle.
