📈 4H EMA Pullback Strategy

Signal: 20 EMA > 50 EMA, both rising, price pulls back to 20 EMA, bullish confirmation candle closes → enter next candle.
Stop: Beyond recent swing high/low, checked against ATR(14).
Target: Minimum 2:1 reward:risk. Trail stop after profit.
When to check: Ideally at 08:05, 12:05, 16:05, 20:05 UK — but you can check any time and still enter mid-candle if price is still near the 20 EMA. The entry quality indicator on each card tells you whether to enter now, set a limit, or wait.

🄐 Darvas Box Strategy — 1H Breakout
Nicolas Darvas method adapted for forex & indices — consolidation, breakout, continuation — 2-5 signals per week
💡 How It Works:

Price consolidates between a box high and box low for at least 3-5 candles.
When price closes above the box high on the 1H chart → BUY breakout signal.
When price closes below the box low on the 1H chart → SELL breakout signal.
Stop: just below the box low (for buys) or above the box high (for sells).
Target: box high + box range size (1:1.5 to 1:2 reward/risk minimum).

Key rule: Only trade boxes where ATR confirms the range is wide enough. Tight boxes = false breakouts. Box must be at least 1x ATR wide to be valid.
▲ BOX BREAKOUT BUY
1H candle closes above box high
Box held for 3+ candles
Box width ≥ 1x ATR
Stop: below box low
Target: box high + range
▼ BOX BREAKOUT SELL
1H candle closes below box low
Box held for 3+ candles
Box width ≥ 1x ATR
Stop: above box high
Target: box low - range
When to check: After each 1H candle closes. Best signals appear during London (08:00-16:00 UK) and NY (13:30-20:00 UK) sessions when volume is highest. Avoid 00:00-07:00 UK — thin markets, false breakouts common.

📆 Previous Day High/Low Strategy
Institutional price memory — yesterday's high and low are the most watched levels by banks and funds
▲ BREAKOUT BUY
1H candle closes above PDH
Strong bullish momentum
Stop: below PDH
Target: PDH + day range
▼ BREAKOUT SELL
1H candle closes below PDL
Strong bearish momentum
Stop: above PDL
Target: PDL - day range
↻ REVERSAL BUY at PDL
Price touches PDL in uptrend
Hammer/engulfing forms
Stop: below PDL (1 ATR)
Target: PDH
↻ REVERSAL SELL at PDH
Price touches PDH in downtrend
Shooting star/engulfing
Stop: above PDH (1 ATR)
Target: PDL
💡 The Logic: Yesterday's high and low are where the most stop-loss orders and pending orders cluster. Banks and algorithms use these levels deliberately — price is magnetically drawn to them. A breakout above PDH means enough buyers overwhelmed all the sellers sitting there. A bounce at PDL means buyers defended the level. Both are high-probability setups when confirmed by a 1H candle close.

⚠ Best during London and NY sessions. Avoid the first 30 minutes after a major news release.
